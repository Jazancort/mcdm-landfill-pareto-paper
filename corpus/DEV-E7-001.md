## Why the previous "root cause" (SAW/vector bug) was wrong

Four contradictions:

1. **Bug claimed in SAW/vector scoring, but TOPSIS/vec also differs (+4.6–5.8 pp).**
   A SAW scorer change cannot alter TOPSIS selection.

2. **SAW/mm/greedy also differs (97.6 vs 95.6).** Min-max does not pass through
   vector normalisation; this cell is unaffected by X_adj vs X_via in vector scoring.

3. **At κ=200, all 16 cells match exactly.** A scorer bug that manifests only at κ=50
   and not at κ=200 cannot be explained by a static function of w.

4. **E7 v2 (with correct k50 matrix) gave identical results to v1 (wrong seed).**
   The E7 v2 log shows no "κ=50 weight matrix SHA OK" line — the new matrix was
   likely NOT loaded. v2 almost certainly ran the old script (seed=43) again.

**Most parsimonious explanation:** mc_2cube and E7 used different seeds for κ=50.
mc_2cube used `np.random.default_rng(42).dirichlet(50×w_ahp, size=500)`.
E7 v1 and v2 both used seed=43. Different draws explain all cell differences,
including min-max and TOPSIS cells.
