# Pre-Specification for WS1: External Validity via Published Decision Matrices

## 1. Purpose

Test whether the ordering Q2(min-max) ≥ Q2(vector) under SAW holds on
published decision matrices from the MCDM literature, and whether the
efficiency loss correlates with ||delta||_2 across instances.

This addresses the main limitation of the paper: "magnitudes are specific to
one candidate set." A corpus of 5-15 real matrices with elicited weights
demonstrates that the mechanism operates outside the spatial geometry of Fogo.

## 2. Inclusion Criteria (pre-declared)

A matrix enters the corpus if and only if ALL of the following hold:

1. **Published:** appeared in a peer-reviewed article or conference proceedings.
2. **Numerical:** the decision matrix X (m alternatives × n criteria) is
   reported with exact numerical values.
3. **Weights declared:** w_j weights are explicitly stated in the article
   (AHP, BWM, SWARA, entropy, equal, or expert judgment — any method is
   acceptable, including equal weights, as long as w_j are tabulated).
4. **Objectives declared:** the benefit/cost direction of each criterion is
   stated or unambiguously inferable.
5. **Minimum size:** n ≥ 3 criteria AND m ≥ 4 alternatives.
6. **No aggregation of criteria:** the raw matrix X (not a pre-normalised or
   pre-weighted version) is available.

Matrices generated by simulation (e.g., Chakraborty 2009) do not qualify.
Survey papers without a worked numerical example do not qualify.
Synthetic/artificial matrices (e.g., Aytekin 2021 Table 5) do not qualify.

## 3. Selection of k (portfolio size)

For each matrix: k = max(3, floor(m/5)), where m = number of alternatives.
This mirrors the Fogo design (k=3 out of m=389 ≈ floor(389/5)=77... too large;
use k=3 universally for comparability since k=3 was Fogo's portfolio size).

**Pre-declared:** k = 3 for all instances.

If m < 6, the instance is kept but flagged as "small-m" and reported separately.

## 4. Objective groupings

Fogo has three groups: G1={slope,roads}, G2={rivers,aspect,coast,protected,wells},
G3={perm,aero,urban,pop}. For corpus instances without a natural grouping,
we use one group per criterion (each criterion is its own objective). This
is the most conservative choice and never inflates Q2 relative to Fogo's
grouped structure.

## 5. Minimum m (pre-declared)

Instances with m < 8 are excluded from the active corpus.
Justification: C(m,3) < 56 trios — insufficient portfolio space for the
greedy/optimal distinction to have content. Without coupling constraints
(no 10km separation rule), the optimal portfolio IS the greedy portfolio
by separability of the additive sum: arg max sum(s_i) = top-k by score.
The assembly factor is therefore zero for any unconstrained corpus instance.

**Scope of the corpus (Mechanism 1 only):** the corpus tests the amplitude
identity and the ordering TV → efficiency loss under SAW scoring, not the
normalisation × assembly interaction. That interaction requires spatial
coupling constraints (as in Fogo) and is tested separately via the
A_j(t) path (WS2).

For unconstrained instances we report k=1 (best alternative) in addition
to k=3, since greedy ≡ optimal by theorem and reporting both is redundant.

Instances excluded by m < 8:
- bhaskar2023_asn (m=4): excluded
- bhaskar2023_agr (m=4): excluded
- chen2020 (m=5): excluded

Active corpus (m ≥ 8):
- milani2005 (m=9) ✓
- vafaei2018 (m=16) ✓
- vafaei2022 (m=6) — BORDERLINE: m=6 < 8, also excluded
- junaid2024 (m=9) ✓

Minimum for publication: 3 confirmed instances with m ≥ 8.
Target: 8-10 instances.

### Primary hypothesis (H1)
For each instance, under SAW with optimal assembly:
  Q2(min-max) ≥ Q2(vector)

Direction: one-sided. Falsified if Q2(min-max) < Q2(vector) on any instance
by more than δ = 2.5 pp (the relevance margin of Section~\ref{sec:design}).

### Secondary hypothesis (H2)
Across instances, the efficiency loss
  Loss = Q2_greedy(min-max) − Q2_greedy(vector)
orders monotonically with ||delta||_2 (Spearman ρ > 0).

### Falsification criterion (pre-declared)
If H1 is violated in ≥ 20% of instances: the mechanism is instance-dependent
and the paper will so state in §8.7.

If H2 gives Spearman ρ ≤ 0: the cross-instance ordering claim is unsupported
and will be removed from the paper.

## 6. Statistical reporting

For each instance:
- Report Q2_greedy and Q2_optimal separately (never only Q2_mean).
- Report TV, m_eff, ||delta||_2 under min-max, vector, and ranks.
- N = 3500 Dirichlet draws (κ=200) per instance.
- Binomial SE = sqrt(Q2*(1-Q2)/N) reported alongside each Q2.

For H2: bootstrap Spearman CI (B=2000 resamples of instances).

## 7. Deviations from this protocol

Any deviation (e.g., k ≠ 3, different grouping, matrix corrected post-extraction)
must be logged in corpus/DEVIATIONS.md with justification.
